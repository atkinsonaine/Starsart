Please read: Commission rules

  • A 50% deposit is required before work begins.
  • The deposit is non-refundable.
  • When contacting me please confirm the artwork details, canvas size, and final price with me before I start.
  • The final artwork may differ slightly from the initial concept.
  • I will provide updates on the artwork's progress throughout the creation process.
  • Please allow 4-6 weeks for the completion of the artwork.
  • If for whatever reason the artwork cannot be completed, the deposit will be refunded.
  • Any additional changes requested after the initial concept is approved may incur extra costs.
  • I reserve the right to refuse any commission request at my discretion.
